# 谐振子例题：含时不确定度

> 通用结果见 [[谐振子]]。下文 $\beta=\sqrt{\dfrac{m\omega}{\hbar}}$，故 $\dfrac{1}{\beta^{2}}=\dfrac{\hbar}{m\omega}$。

**题目**：在 $t=0$ 时刻，一个谐振子处的状态为 $|\psi\rangle=|1\rangle-i|2\rangle$。求坐标的不确定度（即方差涨落）$(\Delta x)^{2}=\langle\hat x^{2}\rangle-\langle\hat x\rangle^{2}$ 随时间的变化。

先归一化：$\langle\psi|\psi\rangle=1+1=2$，故 $|\psi\rangle=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|1\rangle-i|2\rangle)$。

## 方法一：薛定谔绘景

由 $\hat x=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2\,\beta}(\hat a+\hat a^{\dagger})$，$\hat x|n\rangle=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2\,\beta}\big(\sqrt n\,|n-1\rangle+\sqrt{n+1}\,|n+1\rangle\big)$，即

$$
\hat x|1\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\beta}(|0\rangle+\sqrt2|2\rangle),\qquad
\hat x|2\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\beta}(\sqrt2|1\rangle+\sqrt3|3\rangle).
$$

态随时间演化（$E_1=\tfrac32\hbar\omega$，$E_2=\tfrac52\hbar\omega$）

$$
|\psi(t)\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\big(|1\rangle e^{-iE_1t/\hbar}-i|2\rangle e^{-iE_2t/\hbar}\big)=\frac{1}{\sqrt2}e^{-\frac32 i\omega t}\big(|1\rangle-i\,e^{-i\omega t}|2\rangle\big).
$$

整体相位不影响期望，用 $\langle1|\hat x|2\rangle=\langle2|\hat x|1\rangle=\dfrac1\beta$、$\langle1|\hat x|1\rangle=\langle2|\hat x|2\rangle=0$：

$$
\langle\hat x\rangle=\frac12\big[-i\,e^{-i\omega t}\langle1|\hat x|2\rangle+i\,e^{i\omega t}\langle2|\hat x|1\rangle\big]
=\frac12\cdot\frac{i}{\beta}\big(e^{i\omega t}-e^{-i\omega t}\big)=-\frac1\beta\sin\omega t.
$$

故 $\langle\hat x\rangle^{2}=\dfrac{1}{\beta^{2}}\sin^{2}\omega t$。又 $\hat x^{2}=\dfrac{1}{2\beta^{2}}(\hat a^{2}+\hat a\hat a^{\dagger}+\hat a^{\dagger}\hat a+\hat a^{\dagger2})$，

$$
\hat x^{2}|1\rangle=\frac{1}{2\beta^{2}}(3|1\rangle+\sqrt6|3\rangle),\qquad
\hat x^{2}|2\rangle=\frac{1}{2\beta^{2}}(\sqrt2|0\rangle+5|2\rangle+\sqrt{12}|4\rangle).
$$

由于 $\langle1|\hat x^{2}|2\rangle=\langle2|\hat x^{2}|1\rangle=0$，$\langle\hat x^{2}\rangle$ 无时间依赖：

$$
\langle\hat x^{2}\rangle=\frac12\big(\langle1|\hat x^{2}|1\rangle+\langle2|\hat x^{2}|2\rangle\big)=\frac12\Big(\frac{3}{2\beta^{2}}+\frac{5}{2\beta^{2}}\Big)=\frac{2}{\beta^{2}}.
$$

$$
\boxed{\ (\Delta x)^{2}=\langle\hat x^{2}\rangle-\langle\hat x\rangle^{2}=\frac{1}{\beta^{2}}(2-\sin^{2}\omega t)=\frac{1}{\beta^{2}}(1+\cos^{2}\omega t)=\frac{\hbar}{m\omega}(1+\cos^{2}\omega t).\ }
$$

## 方法二：海森堡绘景

固定态 $|\psi\rangle=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(|1\rangle-i|2\rangle)$。由 $\hat H=\hbar\omega(\hat a^{\dagger}\hat a+\tfrac12)$ 及海森堡运动方程

$$
\frac{d\hat a(t)}{dt}=\frac{1}{i\hbar}[\hat a,\hat H]=\frac{1}{i\hbar}\hbar\omega\hat a
\ \Rightarrow\ \hat a(t)=\hat a\,e^{-i\omega t},\quad \hat a^{\dagger}(t)=\hat a^{\dagger}e^{i\omega t}.
$$

故 $\hat x(t)=\sqrt{\dfrac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}\big(\hat a^{\dagger}e^{i\omega t}+\hat a\,e^{-i\omega t}\big)$。用 $\langle\hat a^{\dagger}\rangle=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}i$、$\langle\hat a\rangle=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}i$：

$$
\langle\hat x\rangle=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}\big(\langle\hat a^{\dagger}\rangle e^{i\omega t}+\langle\hat a\rangle e^{-i\omega t}\big)
=\frac12\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{m\omega}}\,i\big(e^{i\omega t}-e^{-i\omega t}\big)=-\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{m\omega}}\sin\omega t,
$$

与方法一一致。又 $\hat x^{2}(t)=\dfrac{1}{2\beta^{2}}\big(\hat a^{2}e^{-2i\omega t}+\hat a^{\dagger2}e^{2i\omega t}+\hat a^{\dagger}\hat a+\hat a\hat a^{\dagger}\big)$，其中 $\langle\hat a^{2}\rangle=\langle\hat a^{\dagger2}\rangle=0$、$\langle\hat a^{\dagger}\hat a\rangle=\dfrac32$、$\langle\hat a\hat a^{\dagger}\rangle=\dfrac52$，故 $\langle\hat x^{2}\rangle=\dfrac{2}{\beta^{2}}$，得到与方法一相同的 $(\Delta x)^{2}=\dfrac{1}{\beta^{2}}(1+\cos^{2}\omega t)$。

## 附：定态 $|n\rangle$ 的不确定关系

对谐振子定态 $|n\rangle$，$\langle\hat x\rangle=\langle\hat p\rangle=0$，可得

$$
\Delta x\,\Delta p=(2n+1)\cdot\frac\hbar2.
$$
