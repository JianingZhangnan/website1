> [!def] Space-inversion Operator
> $$
> \mathcal{P}\ket{x}=\ket{-x}
> $$
> Or, from the **passive** perspective:
> $$
> \langle x|\mathcal{P}^{\dagger}\hat{x}\mathcal{P}|x \rangle =\langle \mathcal{P}x|\hat{x}|\mathcal{P}x \rangle  = \langle -x|\hat{x}| -x \rangle =-\langle x|\hat{x}|x \rangle \implies \mathcal{P}^{\dagger}\hat{x}\mathcal{P}=-\hat{x}
> $$

Now let us consider its action on $\ket{p}$:
$$
\begin{align}
\mathcal{P}\ket{p} &= \int \mathcal{P}\ket{x}\braket{ x | p } \,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \exp\left(\mathrm{i}\frac{p}{\hbar}x\right)\ket{-x}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \exp\left(\mathrm{i}\frac{-p}{\hbar}x'\right)\ket{x'}\,\mathrm{d}x' \qquad (x' = -x) \\
&= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \ket{x'}\braket{x'|-p}\,\mathrm{d}x' = \ket{-p}.
\end{align}
$$

Therefore,

> [!done] Momentum under Space Inversion
> $$
> \mathcal{P}\ket{p}=\ket{-p}
> $$
> Or, from the **passive** perspective:
> $$
> \mathcal{P}^{\dagger}\hat{p}\mathcal{P}=-\hat{p}
> $$
