>[!def] A usual mistake
>$$
>\hat{T}\ket{\psi(t)}=\ket{\psi(-t)}
>$$

This **not** time-reversal, it just put $\ket{\psi }$ to time $-t$, but dont't change the 运动方向。

To obtain the right form, firstly let's make a **fundamental renaming**: the **time** reversal operator, since highly misleading, is actually reversal of **MOTION**. By anchoring this concept, we redefine it as:

>[!def] time reversal operator
> $$
> T:(x,p)\longmapsto (x,-p)
> $$
