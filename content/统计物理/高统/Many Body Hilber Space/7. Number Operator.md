# Number Operator

> [!def] Number operator
> $$
> \hat n_\lambda:=a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda.
> $$
^def-number-operator

From [[6. Commutation Relations#1. If $\lambda=\mu$|the calculation with equal labels]], we know:

> [!done] Basis states are eigenstates of the number operator
> For either Bose or Fermi,
> $$
> \hat n_\lambda|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
> =n_\lambda|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle.
> $$
> Here $n_\lambda$ is the [[3. Basis of Bose and Fermi Spaces#^def-occupation-number|occupation number]] of the basis state.
^prop-number-eigenstate

If $\lambda$ is absent, annihilation gives zero. For Bose, annihilation and creation each contribute $\sqrt{n_\lambda}$, giving $n_\lambda$. For Fermi, the result is the original state if $\lambda$ is present, and zero otherwise; these are exactly $n_\lambda=1$ and $0$.

This eigenvalue equation applies to states with definite $n_\lambda$, including the occupation-number basis. A general superposition need not have one definite $n_\lambda$.

## Properties

The calculations below use the [[6. Commutation Relations#^prop-mixed-relations|creation and annihilation relations]], on the same finite linear combinations of basis states.

### 1. $[\hat n_\lambda,\hat n_\mu]=0$

First, derive the relations between $a_\lambda$ and $\hat n_\mu$. Let $\eta=+1$ for Bose and $\eta=-1$ for Fermi. The relations already proved say

$$
a_\lambda a_\mu^\dagger=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}I+\eta a_\mu^\dagger a_\lambda,
\qquad a_\lambda a_\mu=\eta a_\mu a_\lambda.
$$

Therefore

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_\lambda\hat n_\mu
&=a_\lambda a_\mu^\dagger a_\mu\\
&=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}a_\mu+\eta a_\mu^\dagger a_\lambda a_\mu\\
&=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}a_\mu+a_\mu^\dagger a_\mu a_\lambda\\
&=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}a_\lambda+\hat n_\mu a_\lambda.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $[a_\lambda,\hat n_\mu]=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}a_\lambda$. Taking adjoints gives $[a_\lambda^\dagger,\hat n_\mu]=-\delta_{\lambda,\mu}a_\lambda^\dagger$, using $\hat n_\mu^\dagger=\hat n_\mu$ as shown below.

Now, by the product rule $[AB,C]=A[B,C]+[A,C]B$ (obtained by expanding both sides; see [[对易子与反对易子#对易子|properties of commutator]]),

$$
\begin{aligned}
[\hat n_\lambda,\hat n_\mu]
&=[a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda,\hat n_\mu]\\
&=a_\lambda^\dagger[a_\lambda,\hat n_\mu]
+[a_\lambda^\dagger,\hat n_\mu]a_\lambda\\
&=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda
-\delta_{\lambda,\mu}a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda
=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

> [!done] Number operators commute
> $$
> [\hat n_\lambda,\hat n_\mu]=0.
> $$
^prop-number-commute

### 2. Hermitian

By the [[量子力学/Hilbert空间/简介#^prop-adjoint-algebra|adjoint algebra]],

$$
\hat n_\lambda^\dagger
=(a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda)^\dagger
=a_\lambda^\dagger(a_\lambda^\dagger)^\dagger
=a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda
=\hat n_\lambda.
$$

### 3. Commutation with creation and annihilation

The calculation above gives, for both Bose and Fermi:

> [!done] Number operator relations
> $$
> \hat n_\lambda^\dagger=\hat n_\lambda,\qquad
> [a_\lambda,\hat n_\mu]=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}a_\lambda,\qquad
> [a_\lambda^\dagger,\hat n_\mu]=-\delta_{\lambda,\mu}a_\lambda^\dagger.
> $$
^prop-number-relations

### 4. Vacuum

$$
\hat n_\lambda|0\rangle
=a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda|0\rangle=0.
$$

### 5. $\hat n_\lambda^2=\hat n_\lambda$ for Fermi

By $\{a_\lambda,a_\lambda^\dagger\}=I$ and $a_\lambda^2=(a_\lambda^\dagger)^2=0$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat n_\lambda^2
&=a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda\\
&=a_\lambda^\dagger(I-a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda)a_\lambda\\
&=a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda-(a_\lambda^\dagger)^2a_\lambda^2
=\hat n_\lambda.
\end{aligned}
$$

> [!done] Fermi number operator is a projection
> For Fermi,
> $$
> \hat n_\lambda^2=\hat n_\lambda.
> $$
> Its occupation-number eigenvalues are $0$ and $1$.
^prop-fermi-number-projection

## Total number operator

> [!def] Total number operator
> $$
> \hat N:=\sum_\lambda\hat n_\lambda
> =\sum_\lambda a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda.
> $$
^def-total-number-operator

Since $\sum_\lambda n_\lambda=N$ in an $N$-particle basis state,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat N|n_1\cdots n_i\cdots\rangle
&=\sum_\lambda\hat n_\lambda|n_1\cdots n_i\cdots\rangle\\
&=\left(\sum_\lambda n_\lambda\right)|n_1\cdots n_i\cdots\rangle
=N|n_1\cdots n_i\cdots\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

> [!done] Total number on the vacuum and fixed-particle-number spaces
> $$
> \hat N|0\rangle=0,\qquad
> \hat N|\psi_N\rangle=N|\psi_N\rangle,
> $$
> where $|\psi_N\rangle$ belongs to $\mathcal B^N$ or $\mathcal F^N$.
^prop-total-number
