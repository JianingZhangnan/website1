# Creation and Annihilation

The normalized basis states and their ordering conventions are those in [[3. Basis of Bose and Fermi Spaces#Constructing the basis|the construction of the Bose and Fermi bases]]. Write $n_\lambda$ for the occupation number of $\lambda$ before an operator acts.

## Creation

> [!def] Creation operator $a_\lambda^\dagger$
> On the normalized basis states, define
> $$
> a_\lambda^\dagger|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
> =\sqrt{n_\lambda+1}\,|\lambda\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle,
> \qquad a_\lambda^\dagger|0\rangle=|\lambda\rangle.
> $$
> Extend this action linearly. In the Fermi case, a ket with a repeated label is zero.
^def-creation-operator

In the Bose case,

$$
a_\lambda^\dagger|n_1\cdots n_\lambda\cdots\rangle
=\sqrt{n_\lambda+1}\,|n_1\cdots(n_\lambda+1)\cdots\rangle.
$$

In the Fermi case,

$$
a_\lambda^\dagger|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
=\begin{cases}
|\lambda\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle,&\lambda\notin\{\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_N\},\\
0,&\lambda\in\{\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_N\}.
\end{cases}
$$

The newly inserted label is written at the front. Reordering a Fermi ket into the fixed basis order may introduce a minus sign.

By induction, we obtain:

> [!done] Constructing basis states from the vacuum
> $$
> |\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
> =\dfrac1{\sqrt{\prod_\lambda n_\lambda!}}\,
> a_{\lambda_1}^\dagger a_{\lambda_2}^\dagger\cdots a_{\lambda_N}^\dagger|0\rangle.
> $$
> For Bose, this is also
> $$
> |n_1n_2\cdots\rangle
> =\dfrac1{\sqrt{\prod_\lambda n_\lambda!}}\,
> (a_1^\dagger)^{n_1}(a_2^\dagger)^{n_2}\cdots|0\rangle.
> $$
> For Fermi, all nonzero occupations are $1$, so the factorial factor is $1$. Notice the order of multiplication.
^prop-creation-from-vacuum

Each repeated creation in state $\lambda$ contributes a factor $\sqrt1,\sqrt2,\ldots,\sqrt{n_\lambda}$; their product is $\sqrt{n_\lambda!}$. Operators act from right to left, so the written order of the creation operators produces the written order of the Fermi labels.

## Annihilation

> [!def] Annihilation operator $a_\lambda$
> Let $a_\lambda=(a_\lambda^\dagger)^\dagger$. Thus
> $$
> \langle\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N|a_\lambda
> =\sqrt{n_\lambda+1}\,\langle\lambda\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N|.
> $$
^def-annihilation-operator

Taking the adjoint uses the [[量子力学/Hilbert空间/简介#^prop-adjoint-algebra|adjoint algebra]]: the order of a product is reversed, and taking the adjoint twice returns the original operator.

Now let us think about how annihilation acts on kets. Insert the [[4. Fock Space#^prop-fock-completeness|completeness relation in Fock space]]:

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_\lambda|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=\sum_{N'=0}^{\infty}\sum_{\{\lambda'_i\}}'
|\lambda'_1\cdots\lambda'_{N'}\rangle
\langle\lambda'_1\cdots\lambda'_{N'}|a_\lambda|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle\\
&=\sum_{N'=0}^{\infty}\sum_{\{\lambda'_i\}}'
\sqrt{n'_\lambda+1}\,|\lambda'_1\cdots\lambda'_{N'}\rangle
\langle\lambda\lambda'_1\cdots\lambda'_{N'}|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

Here the prime on the sum means independent basis states only, and $n'_\lambda$ is the occupation number of $\lambda$ in the primed ket.

### 1. If $N=0$

The overlap in the last line is zero: the bra contains at least one particle, while the ket is the vacuum. So

$$
a_\lambda|0\rangle=0.
$$

### 2. If $N=1$

Only $N'=0$ can contribute, and the remaining overlap is $\langle\lambda|\mu\rangle=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}$. So

$$
a_\lambda|\mu\rangle=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}|0\rangle.
$$

### 3. If $N>1$

Only $N'=N-1$ can contribute. The overlap is the Bose occupation-number delta or the signed Fermi delta in [[3. Basis of Bose and Fermi Spaces#Constructing the basis|the basis inner products]]. A nonzero term must have the original occupation list with one $\lambda$ removed.

For Bose,

> [!done] Annihilation on the Bose basis
> $$
> a_\lambda|n_1\cdots n_\lambda\cdots\rangle
> =\begin{cases}
> \sqrt{n_\lambda}\,|n_1\cdots(n_\lambda-1)\cdots\rangle,&n_\lambda>0,\\
> 0,&n_\lambda=0.
> \end{cases}
> $$
^prop-bose-annihilation

Indeed, the only matching independent basis state has $n'_\lambda=n_\lambda-1$, so $\sqrt{n'_\lambda+1}=\sqrt{n_\lambda}$.

The notation $|\lambda_1\cdots\widehat{\lambda_i}\cdots\lambda_N\rangle$ means deleting the indicated entry. For example,

$$
a_\beta|\alpha\alpha\beta\beta\beta\mu\rangle
=\sqrt3\,|\alpha\alpha\beta\beta\mu\rangle.
$$

For Fermi, if $\lambda$ is in slot $i$, the overlap is obtained by moving it to the front, across $i-1$ labels. This gives $(-1)^{i-1}$.

> [!done] Annihilation on the Fermi basis
> $$
> a_\lambda|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
> =\begin{cases}
> (-1)^{i-1}|\lambda_1\cdots\widehat{\lambda_i}\cdots\lambda_N\rangle,
> &\lambda=\lambda_i,\\
> 0,&\lambda\notin\{\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_N\}.
> \end{cases}
> $$
> Here $i$ is the position of $\lambda$ in the written list.
^prop-fermi-annihilation

In summary, we obtain:

> [!done] Unified annihilation formula
> With the upper sign for Bose and the lower sign for Fermi,
> $$
> a_\lambda|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
> =\begin{cases}
> \dfrac1{\sqrt{n_\lambda}}\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}(\pm)^{i-1}
> \delta_{\lambda,\lambda_i}
> |\lambda_1\cdots\widehat{\lambda_i}\cdots\lambda_N\rangle,&n_\lambda>0,\\
> 0,&n_\lambda=0.
> \end{cases}
> $$
^prop-unified-annihilation

For Bose, the $n_\lambda$ equal contributions give $n_\lambda/\sqrt{n_\lambda}=\sqrt{n_\lambda}$. For Fermi, there is at most one contribution and $n_\lambda=1$.
