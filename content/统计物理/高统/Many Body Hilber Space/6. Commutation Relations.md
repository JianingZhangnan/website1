# Commutation relations

Use the [[5. Creation and Annihilation#^def-creation-operator|creation operator]] and [[5. Creation and Annihilation#^prop-unified-annihilation|annihilation formula]] already constructed. The brackets are the [[对易子与反对易子#对易子|commutator]] $[A,B]=AB-BA$ and the [[对易子与反对易子#反对易子|anticommutator]] $\{A,B\}=AB+BA$.

The following calculations are on finite linear combinations of basis states, where all the operator products used here are defined.

## 1. Creations themselves

Consider $a_\lambda^\dagger a_\mu^\dagger|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle$.

### Bose

If $\lambda\neq\mu$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_\lambda^\dagger a_\mu^\dagger|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=\sqrt{n_\mu+1}\sqrt{n_\lambda+1}\,
|\lambda\mu\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle,\\
a_\mu^\dagger a_\lambda^\dagger|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=\sqrt{n_\lambda+1}\sqrt{n_\mu+1}\,
|\mu\lambda\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

For Bose, $|\lambda\mu\cdots\rangle=|\mu\lambda\cdots\rangle$, so the two results agree. If $\lambda=\mu$, both products are the same operator; the factor is $\sqrt{(n_\lambda+1)(n_\lambda+2)}$. Thus $[a_\lambda^\dagger,a_\mu^\dagger]=0$.

### Fermi

If $\lambda\neq\mu$ and neither state is occupied,

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_\lambda^\dagger a_\mu^\dagger|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=|\lambda\mu\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle,\\
a_\mu^\dagger a_\lambda^\dagger|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=|\mu\lambda\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
=-|\lambda\mu\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

If either is occupied, both products are zero. If $\lambda=\mu$, $(a_\lambda^\dagger)^2=0$ by the [[3. Basis of Bose and Fermi Spaces#^prop-pauli-exclusion|Pauli exclusion principle]]. Hence $\{a_\lambda^\dagger,a_\mu^\dagger\}=0$ for all $\lambda,\mu$.

Taking adjoints gives the corresponding relations for annihilation operators.

> [!done] Relations among creations, and among annihilations
> Bose:
> $$
> [a_\lambda^\dagger,a_\mu^\dagger]=0,\qquad[a_\lambda,a_\mu]=0.
> $$
> Fermi:
> $$
> \{a_\lambda^\dagger,a_\mu^\dagger\}=0,\qquad\{a_\lambda,a_\mu\}=0.
> $$
^prop-creation-annihilation-separate

## 2. Creation and annihilation

### 1. If $\lambda=\mu$

For Bose, the raising and lowering factors give

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_\lambda a_\lambda^\dagger|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=(n_\lambda+1)|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle,\\
a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=n_\lambda|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

The second result is zero for $n_\lambda=0$. So $[a_\lambda,a_\lambda^\dagger]=I$.

For Fermi,

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_\lambda a_\lambda^\dagger|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=\begin{cases}
0,&n_\lambda=1,\\
|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle,&n_\lambda=0,
\end{cases}\\
a_\lambda^\dagger a_\lambda|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=\begin{cases}
|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle,&n_\lambda=1,\\
0,&n_\lambda=0.
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$

When $n_\lambda=1$, annihilation removes $\lambda$ from slot $i$ with sign $(-1)^{i-1}$. Creation puts it at the front; restoring its original position gives another $(-1)^{i-1}$, so the two signs cancel. Thus $\{a_\lambda,a_\lambda^\dagger\}=I$.

### 2. If $\lambda\neq\mu$

If $n_\lambda=0$, both products $a_\lambda a_\mu^\dagger$ and $a_\mu^\dagger a_\lambda$ give zero.

If $n_\lambda>0$, creating $\mu$ does not change $n_\lambda$, and removing $\lambda$ does not change $n_\mu$. In the nonzero cases, the two orders give

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_\lambda a_\mu^\dagger|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=\dfrac{\sqrt{n_\mu+1}}{\sqrt{n_\lambda}}
\sum_{i=1}^{N}(\pm)^i\delta_{\lambda,\lambda_i}
|\mu\lambda_1\cdots\widehat{\lambda_i}\cdots\lambda_N\rangle,\\
a_\mu^\dagger a_\lambda|\lambda_1\cdots\lambda_N\rangle
&=\dfrac{\sqrt{n_\mu+1}}{\sqrt{n_\lambda}}
\sum_{i=1}^{N}(\pm)^{i-1}\delta_{\lambda,\lambda_i}
|\mu\lambda_1\cdots\widehat{\lambda_i}\cdots\lambda_N\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

In the first line, the newly created $\mu$ shifts the position of the removed $\lambda$ by one. This is the extra factor $\pm$. In the Fermi case, if $\mu$ is already occupied, both orders give zero by exclusion.

So $[a_\lambda,a_\mu^\dagger]=0$ for Bose and $\{a_\lambda,a_\mu^\dagger\}=0$ for Fermi when $\lambda\neq\mu$.

In summary:

> [!done] Creation and annihilation relations
> Bose:
> $$
> [a_\lambda,a_\mu^\dagger]=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}I.
> $$
> Fermi:
> $$
> \{a_\lambda,a_\mu^\dagger\}=\delta_{\lambda,\mu}I.
> $$
>
> Equivalently,
> $$
> a_\mu a_\lambda^\dagger
> =\delta_{\mu\lambda}I+\eta a_\lambda^\dagger a_\mu,
> \qquad
> \eta=\begin{cases}
> +1,&\text{Bose},\\
> -1,&\text{Fermi}.
> \end{cases}
> $$
^prop-mixed-relations

Since these identities hold on every basis state, they hold on their finite linear combinations.
