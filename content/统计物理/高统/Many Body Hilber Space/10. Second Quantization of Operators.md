# Second Quantization of Operators

In the [[4. Fock Space#^prop-fock-completeness|independent Fock basis]], an operator has the expansion

$$
A=\sum_{N,M}\sum_{\alpha,\beta}
A_{N\alpha,M\beta}|N,\alpha\rangle\langle M,\beta|,
\qquad A_{N\alpha,M\beta}=\langle N,\alpha|A|M,\beta\rangle.
$$

Here $\alpha,\beta$ label independent normalized states in their respective particle-number sectors. Particle-number conservation means that $A$ preserves each particle-number sector. Thus $P_NAP_M=0$ for $N\ne M$, leaving only $N=M$ blocks. The sums are read on states for which the operator expansion is defined.

For one-particle labels, the [[5. Creation and Annihilation#2. If $N=1$|single-particle annihilation rule]] gives

$$
P_1a_\lambda^\dagger a_\mu P_1|\nu\rangle
=\delta_{\mu\nu}|\lambda\rangle,
\qquad
|\lambda\rangle\langle\mu|=P_1a_\lambda^\dagger a_\mu P_1.
$$

Here $P_1$ projects onto the one-particle sector; both sides of the second equality vanish on all other sectors.

> [!def] One-body operator
> Let $f:\mathcal H\to\mathcal H$ have matrix elements $f_{\lambda\mu}=\langle\lambda|f|\mu\rangle$. On the $N$-particle product space, set
> $$
> f_i=I^{\otimes(i-1)}\otimes f\otimes I^{\otimes(N-i)},
> \qquad T_N=\sum_{i=1}^N f_i.
> $$
> The corresponding one-body operator on Fock space acts as $T_N$ restricted to the Bose or Fermi sector $\mathcal X^N$, and as zero on the vacuum.
^def-one-body-total-operator

The slot convention is that of [[1. Basic Space#^def-basic-space|the product space]]. Its one-particle block must be

$$
f=\sum_{\lambda,\mu}f_{\lambda\mu}|\lambda\rangle\langle\mu|
=\left.P_1\left(\sum_{\lambda,\mu}f_{\lambda\mu}a_\lambda^\dagger a_\mu\right)P_1\right|_{\mathcal X^1}.
$$

This suggests the candidate

$$
F:=\sum_{\lambda,\mu}f_{\lambda\mu}a_\lambda^\dagger a_\mu.
$$

We must check every $N$-particle block, not just remove the projections. For the proof, take a finite-dimensional single-particle space and finite particle-number expansions.

## 1. Action of the candidate

Write $|\chi_N\rangle=a_{\mu_1}^\dagger\cdots a_{\mu_N}^\dagger|0\rangle$, an unnormalized creation string. Using $a_\nu|0\rangle=0$ from [[5. Creation and Annihilation#1. If $N=0$|vacuum annihilation]], apply [[6a. Annihilation through a Creation String#^prop-annihilation-through-creation-string|the creation-string identity]] with $b=a_\nu$ and $c_\mu=\delta_{\nu\mu}$:

$$
F|\chi_N\rangle
=\sum_{i=1}^N\sum_\lambda f_{\lambda\mu_i}\eta^{i-1}
a_\lambda^\dagger a_{\mu_1}^\dagger\cdots\widehat{a_{\mu_i}^\dagger}\cdots a_{\mu_N}^\dagger|0\rangle.
$$

Here $\eta=+1$ for Bose and $-1$ for Fermi; a hat denotes omission. By [[6. Commutation Relations#^prop-creation-annihilation-separate|the creation–creation relation]], moving $a_\lambda^\dagger$ back to position $i$ gives another $\eta^{i-1}$. Their product is $1$, hence

$$
F|\chi_N\rangle
=\sum_{i=1}^N\sum_\lambda f_{\lambda\mu_i}
a_{\mu_1}^\dagger\cdots a_{\mu_{i-1}}^\dagger a_\lambda^\dagger a_{\mu_{i+1}}^\dagger\cdots a_{\mu_N}^\dagger|0\rangle.
\tag{1}
$$

## 2. Relating the string to a product state

Let $S=S_b$ or $S_f$ be the [[2. Symmetrizer and Antisymmetrizer#^def-symmetrizer|appropriate symmetrizer]]. By [[5. Creation and Annihilation#^prop-creation-from-vacuum|basis construction]],

$$
|\chi_N\rangle=\sqrt D\,|\mu_1\cdots\mu_N\rangle,
\qquad D=\prod_\mu n_\mu!,
$$

where $n_\mu$ counts occurrences of $\mu$ ([[3. Basis of Bose and Fermi Spaces#^def-occupation-number|occupation number]]). The [[3. Basis of Bose and Fermi Spaces#^def-bose-basis|Bose normalization]] gives $|\mu_1\cdots\mu_N\rangle=\sqrt{N!/D}\,S_b|\mu_1\cdots\mu_N)$. The [[3. Basis of Bose and Fermi Spaces#^def-fermi-basis|Fermi normalization]] gives the same relation with $D=1$ for distinct labels. Repeated Fermi labels make both sides zero. Thus

$$
|\chi_N\rangle=\sqrt{N!}\,S|\mu_1\cdots\mu_N).
\tag{2}
$$

This identity also holds after replacing any label, even if the occupation numbers change.

## 3. Independent calculation with particle slots

A [[1. Basic Space#^def-permutation-operator|permutation]] $P$ merely relabels the slots in $T_N$, so

$$
PT_NP^{-1}=\sum_i Pf_iP^{-1}=\sum_i f_i=T_N.
$$

Since $S$ is a linear combination of permutations, $T_NS=ST_N$. Using (2),

$$
T_N|\chi_N\rangle=\sqrt{N!}\,ST_N|\mu_1\cdots\mu_N).
$$

The definition of $f_i$ and $f|\mu_i\rangle=\sum_\lambda f_{\lambda\mu_i}|\lambda\rangle$ give

$$
T_N|\mu_1\cdots\mu_N)
=\sum_{i=1}^N\sum_\lambda f_{\lambda\mu_i}
|\mu_1\cdots\mu_{i-1}\lambda\mu_{i+1}\cdots\mu_N).
$$

Apply $\sqrt{N!}S$ and then use (2) on each replaced list:

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_N|\chi_N\rangle
&=\sum_{i,\lambda}f_{\lambda\mu_i}\sqrt{N!}\,S|\mu_1\cdots\mu_{i-1}\lambda\mu_{i+1}\cdots\mu_N)\\
&=\sum_{i,\lambda}f_{\lambda\mu_i}
a_{\mu_1}^\dagger\cdots a_{\mu_{i-1}}^\dagger a_\lambda^\dagger a_{\mu_{i+1}}^\dagger\cdots a_{\mu_N}^\dagger|0\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is exactly (1). These strings span $\mathcal X^N$ by the basis construction, so linearity gives equality on that sector. Also $F|0\rangle=0$.

> [!done] One-body operator in creation–annihilation form
> Under the assumptions above,
> $$
> F=\sum_{\lambda,\mu}f_{\lambda\mu}a_\lambda^\dagger a_\mu,
> \qquad F|0\rangle=0,
> \qquad \left.F\right|_{\mathcal X^N}=\left.\sum_{i=1}^N f_i\right|_{\mathcal X^N}.
> $$
> For infinite-dimensional single-particle spaces, the formula requires domains and convergence on which both expressions are defined.
^prop-one-body-second-quantization

In the block diagram, $P_1FP_1=0\oplus f\oplus0\oplus\cdots$. The full $F$ includes all higher-particle blocks; their equality to the slot sums was proved separately above.
