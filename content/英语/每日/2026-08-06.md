>[!example]- leverage
>- UK. /ˈliːvərɪdʒ/ | US. /ˈlevərɪdʒ/
>- 源于lever(杠杆)。阿基米德名言：Give me a lever long enough, and I shall move the world. 
>- 意思是充分利用、发挥……的优势
>- 例句：
>	- Additionally, it leverages subband feature extraction modules and temporal recurrent attention modules to enhance its performance.
>	- The sensor leverages the quantum tunneling effect to measure tiny displacements.

>[!example]- incorporate
>- UK.`/ɪnˈkɔːpəreɪt/` | US. `/ɪnˈkɔːrpəreɪt/`
>- 将……纳入，包含
>- 例句
>	- In this experiment, we need to incorporate air resistance into the equation of motion.
>	- The new model incorporates quantum effects to explain the anomaly
