# Basic Definations

>[!def] homomorphism
>Let $G$ and $G'$ be groups, written with multiplicative notation. A *homomorphism* $\varphi:G\to G'$ is a map from $G$ to $G'$ such that for all $a$ and $b$ in G,
>$$
>\varphi(ab) = \varphi(a)\varphi(b)
>$$

^group-homomorphism

等式左边先在 $G$ 中相乘，再映到 $G'$；右边先分别映到 $G'$，再使用 $G'$ 的运算。两边的乘法未必是同一种具体操作，例如可以从整数加法映到复数乘法。

> [!def] 交换图表
> 用箭头表示映射时，若从同一起点到同一终点的不同路径给出相同的复合映射，称图表**交换**（commutative diagram）。
> 对同态，其含义是 $\varphi\circ m=m'\circ(\varphi\times\varphi)$，其中 $(\varphi\times\varphi)(g,h)=(\varphi(g),\varphi(h))$。

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
G\times G&\xrightarrow{\ m\ }&G\\
{\scriptstyle\varphi\times\varphi}\downarrow&&\downarrow{\scriptstyle\varphi}\\
G'\times G'&\xrightarrow{\ m'\ }&G'
\end{array}
$$

>[!example]- 
>- the determinant function det: $GL_{n}(\mathbf{F})\to \mathbf{F}^{\times },\quad \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{C},\mathbf{R},\mathbf{Q}$ any field.
>-  the sign homomorphism $\sigma:S_{n}\to \{ +1,-1 \}$ that sends a permutation to its sign.
>- the exponential map exp: $\mathbb{R}^{+}\to \mathbb{R}^{\times }$ defined by $x\to \mathrm{e}^{ x }$

>[!done]- Proposition 2.5.3
>Let $\varphi : G \to G'$ be a group homomorphism.
>
>(a) If $a_1, \dots, a_k$ are elements of $G$, then $\varphi(a_1 \cdots a_k) = \varphi(a_1) \cdots \varphi(a_k)$.  
>(b) $\varphi$ maps the identity to the identity: $\varphi(1_G) = 1_{G'}$.  
>(c) $\varphi$ maps inverses to inverses: $\varphi(a^{-1}) = \varphi(a)^{-1}$.

^homomorphism-properties

证明不需另加保持单位元的假设。由 $\varphi(e)=\varphi(ee)=\varphi(e)^2$，在目标群中消去一个 $\varphi(e)$，得到 $\varphi(e)=e'$。随后

$$
e'=\varphi(gg^{-1})=\varphi(g)\varphi(g^{-1}),
$$

所以 $\varphi(g^{-1})=\varphi(g)^{-1}$。多个因子的公式通过重复使用同态条件得到。

>[!def] image and kernel
>- The *image* of a homomorphism $\varphi: G \to G'$, often denoted by $\mathrm{im}\,\varphi$, is simply the image of $\varphi$ as a map of sets:
>$$
>\mathrm{im}\,\varphi = \{ x \in G' \mid x = \varphi(a) \text{ for some } a \text{ in } G \}, \tag{2.5.4}
>$$
>Another notation for the image would be $\varphi(G)$.
>
>- The *kernel* of $\varphi$, often denoted by $\ker \varphi$, is the set of elements of $G$ that are mapped to the identity in $G'$:
>$$
>\ker \varphi = \{ a \in G \mid \varphi(a) = 1 \}. \tag{2.5.5}
>$$
>The kernel contains $1_G$; for $a,b\in\ker\varphi$, $\varphi(ab^{-1})=\varphi(a)\varphi(b)^{-1}=1_{G'}$. Hence it is a **subgroup** by the subgroup criterion (GT-ch03, Proposition 3.9).

^kernel-image

> [!done] Image of a homomorphism is a subgroup
> The image is a subgroup of $G'$: it contains $1_{G'}=\varphi(1_G)$; if $x=\varphi(a)$ and $y=\varphi(b)$ are in $\operatorname{im}\varphi$, then $xy=\varphi(ab)$ is also in the image; and $x^{-1}=\varphi(a^{-1})$ is in the image as well.

^image-subgroup

This recovers two familiar concepts from linear algebra. A [[1B Vector Space|vector space]] $V$ over a field $\mathbf{F}$ is an abelian group $(V,+)$: its identity is $0$, and the inverse of $v$ is $-v$; scalar multiplication is its additional structure. Hence a linear map $T:V\to W$ is also a homomorphism of additive groups because $T(u+v)=T(u)+T(v)$. Under this viewpoint, $\ker T$ is exactly the [[3B Null Spaces and Ranges#^862fcf|null space]] of $T$, and $\operatorname{im}T$ is its [[3B Null Spaces and Ranges#^b78918|range]].

>[!example] kernels of familiar homomorphisms
>- The kernel of the determinant homomorphism $\det:GL_n(\mathbf{R})\to\mathbf{R}^{\times}$ is the special linear group
>$$
>SL_n(\mathbf{R})=\{A\in GL_n(\mathbf{R})\mid \det A=1\}.
>$$
>- The kernel of the sign homomorphism $\sigma:S_n\to\{\pm1\}$ is the alternating group $A_n$.

>[!def] alternating group $A_n$ (2.5.6)
>The *alternating group* $A_n$ is the group of even permutations, meaning the permutations whose sign is $+1$. Equivalently,
>$$
>A_n=\ker\sigma.
>$$

# Coset

>[!def] left coset (2.5.7)
>If $H$ is a subgroup of a group $G$ and $a\in G$, then
>$$
>aH=\{g\in G\mid g=ah\text{ for some }h\in H\}.
>$$
>This set is called a *left coset* of $H$ in $G$. The word “left” refers to the fact that $a$ appears on the left. Similarly, we can define *right coset*.

^692053

>[!done] Proposition 2.5.8
>Let $\varphi:G\to G'$ be a group homomorphism, let $K=\ker\varphi$, and let $a,b\in G$. The following conditions are equivalent:
>- $\varphi(a)=\varphi(b)$;
>- $a^{-1}b\in K$;
>- $b\in aK$;
>- $aK=bK$.

**Proof** Suppose that $\varphi(a)=\varphi(b)$. Then
$$
\varphi(a^{-1}b)=\varphi(a^{-1})\varphi(b)=\varphi(a)^{-1}\varphi(b)=1,
$$
so $a^{-1}b\in K$. Conversely, if $a^{-1}b\in K$, then
$$
1=\varphi(a^{-1}b)=\varphi(a)^{-1}\varphi(b),
$$
so $\varphi(a)=\varphi(b)$. This proves the equivalence of the first two conditions.

By the definition of a left coset,
$$
a^{-1}b\in K\iff b=a(a^{-1}b)\in aK,
$$
so the second and third conditions are equivalent. If $b=ak_0\in aK$, then $bK=ak_0K=aK$ because $k_0\in K$; conversely, if $bK=aK$, then $b=b1_G\in bK=aK$. Thus the third and fourth conditions, and hence all four conditions, are equivalent.

> [!def] 单射、满射、双射（Definition 3.2）
> 对映射 $f:X\to Y$：若 $f(x_1)=f(x_2)$ 必有 $x_1=x_2$，称为**单射**（injective）；若每个 $y\in Y$ 都等于某个 $f(x)$，称为**满射**（surjective）；兼具两者称为**双射**（bijective）。

>[!done] Corollary 2.5.9
>A homomorphism $\varphi:G\to G'$ is injective if and only if
>$$
>\ker\varphi=\{1_G\}.
>$$

^injective-kernel

**Proof** If $\ker\varphi=\{1_G\}$ and $\varphi(a)=\varphi(b)$, Proposition 2.5.8 gives $a^{-1}b=1_G$, hence $a=b$; therefore $\varphi$ is injective. Conversely, if $\varphi$ is injective and $a\in\ker\varphi$, then $\varphi(a)=1_{G'}=\varphi(1_G)$, so $a=1_G$. Therefore $\ker\varphi=\{1_G\}$.

---

>[!def] Normal Subgroup
>A Subgroup $N$ of a group $G$ is called a *normal subgroup* if for every $a$ in $N$ and $g$ in $G$, the conjugate $gag^{-1}\in N$.

^normal-subgroup

Clearly, every subgroup of an **abelian** group is normal. Besides, the [[1. Basic Definitions and Examples#^a42899|quaternion group]] is a classic non-abelian group whose subgroups are all normal.

> [!done] 同态的核对共轭封闭（Quiz 3.1）
> 对 $k\in\ker\varphi$、$g\in G$，$gkg^{-1}\in\ker\varphi$。因此核是 [[Group/1. Foundations/2. Homomorphism#^normal-subgroup|正规子群]]。

直接计算

$$
\varphi(gkg^{-1})=\varphi(g)\varphi(k)\varphi(g)^{-1}
=\varphi(g)e'\varphi(g)^{-1}=e'.
$$

这里暂只使用正规性的这一条件；商群的一般构造属于后续课程。

# Fibers

>[!def] Fiber, inverse image
>The *inverse image* or *fiber* of an element $t$ of $T$ is the subset of $S$ consisting of all elements $s$ such that $f(s)=t$. It is denoted symbolically as
> $$
> f^{-1}(t) = \{ s\in S \ \big| \ f(s) = t \}
> $$

![[Algebra, Second Edition.pdf#page=67&rect=108,68,353,219|Algebra, Second Edition, p.67]]

![[Algebra, Second Edition.pdf#page=68&rect=61,262,412,449|Algebra, Second Edition, p.68]]

We call it **fiber** because the diagram above shows it looks very much like one.

>[!done] coset and fiber
>Let $K$ be the kernel of a homomorphism $\varphi:G\to G'$. The fiber of $\varphi$ that contains an element $a$ of $G$ is the coset $aK$ of $K$. These cosets partition the group $G$, and they correspond to elements of the image of $\varphi$ .

**Proof** We need to check that if $\varphi(b)=\varphi (a)$, then there is a $k\in K$, such that $b=ak$. 
Let's consider that:
$$
\varphi(a^{-1}b) = \varphi(a)^{-1}\varphi(b) = \varphi(a)^{-1}\varphi(a) = 1
$$
So $a^{-1}b\in K$. Set $k=a^{-1}b$; then $b=ak\in aK$. Conversely, $b=ak$ with $k\in K$ gives $\varphi(b)=\varphi(a)\varphi(k)=\varphi(a)$.

>[!done] Index of the kernel equals the order of the image
>$$
>[G:\mathrm{ker}\ \varphi ] = |\mathrm{im}\ \varphi |
>$$


# Homomorphism Examples

## 幂映射（Example 3.12）

> [!def] 单位根群的幂映射
> 对整数 $k$，定义 $P_k:\mu_N\to\mu_N$，$P_k(z)=z^k$。

> [!done] 幂映射的核、像与同构条件
> 设 $d=\gcd(k,N)$。则 $P_k$ 是同态，$\ker P_k=\mu_d$，$\operatorname{im}P_k=\mu_{N/d}$；它是同构当且仅当 $d=1$。

同态性来自单位根的交换律。写 $z=\omega^r$，则 $z^k=1$ 等价于 $N\mid kr$，又等价于 $N/d\mid r$。所以核为 $\{e^{2\pi i\ell/d}:0\le\ell<d\}=\mu_d$。像由 $\omega^k$ 生成，它的阶为 $N/d$，因此恰为 $\mu_{N/d}$。$k=0$ 时 $d=N$，仍符合公式。

$N=4,k=2$ 时，$1,i,-1,-i$ 分别映到 $1,-1,1,-1$，核与像均为 $\{1,-1\}$。$P_N$ 则是把所有元素送到 $1$ 的平凡同态。这是 [[Group/1. Foundations/Isomorphism#循环群的全部自同构（Example 3.7）|乘法映射 $m_k$]] 经 [[Group/1. Foundations/Isomorphism#剩余类与单位根（Example 3.11）|剩余类与单位根同构]] 改写后的乘法版本。

## 圆群到特殊酉群（Example 3.13）

固定整数 $N\ge2$，考虑

$$
\varphi:U(1)\longrightarrow SU(2),\qquad
z\longmapsto\begin{pmatrix}z^N&0\\0&z^{-N}\end{pmatrix}.
$$

因 $|z|=1$，两个对角元的模都为 $1$，行列式为 $1$，确实落在 $SU(2)$；相乘时对角元各自相乘，故为同态。核条件为 $z^N=1$，给出 $\ker\varphi=\mu_N$。任意 $w\in U(1)$ 都有一个 $N$ 次根，因此

$$
\operatorname{im}\varphi=
\left\{\begin{pmatrix}w&0\\0&w^{-1}\end{pmatrix}:|w|=1\right\}.
$$

这个像通过读取第一对角元与 $U(1)$ 同构，但只包含对角矩阵，例如 $\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\in SU(2)$ 不在像中。故原映射既不单射，也不满射。

# Matrix Realizations

以下 $\kappa=\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。[[Group/1. Foundations/Isomorphism#从可逆线性变换到可逆矩阵（Example 3.20）|选基给出 $GL(V)\cong GL(n,\kappa)$]]，从而可以用具体矩阵研究群同态。

## 由生成元构造矩阵同态

> [!def] 群的矩阵实现
> 一个同态 $\rho:G\to GL(n,\kappa)$ 称为本页所说的**矩阵实现**，也称 $n$ 维线性表示。若 $\rho$ 单射，则 $G$ 与矩阵子群 $\operatorname{im}\rho$ 同构；一般同态允许不同群元素被送到同一矩阵。

> [!done] 用群展示定义矩阵同态
> 若 $G=\langle g_1,\ldots,g_s\mid R_1,\ldots,R_t\rangle$ 是完整的群展示，且可逆矩阵 $M_1,\ldots,M_s$ 满足相同的定义关系，那么赋值 $g_i\mapsto M_i$ 唯一延拓为同态 $G\to GL(n,\kappa)$。

把每个表示群元素的字串中的 $g_i$ 换成 $M_i$，逆元换成矩阵逆。定义关系和消去操作在矩阵端都成立，所以同一个群元素的不同字串给出同一个矩阵，映射良定义；拼接字串对应矩阵相乘，故为同态；生成元的像已固定，延拓唯一。

只验证关系能保证存在同态，是否单射还要检查核或数清不同的像。

## 四种矩阵实现（Example 3.21）

### 整数加法群

固定 $a\in\kappa$，令

$$
\varphi_a:\mathbb Z\to GL(2,\kappa),\qquad
n\longmapsto\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\na&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

矩阵行列式为 $1$，且

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\na&1\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\ma&1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\(n+m)a&1\end{pmatrix},
$$

所以是同态。$a\ne0$ 时，因本页的域是实数或复数，$na=0$ 只在 $n=0$ 时发生，核平凡，因此 $\mathbb Z\cong\operatorname{im}\varphi_a$。$a=0$ 时则所有整数都映到 $I_2$，核为整个 $\mathbb Z$。这里不能把 $a\ne0$ 的单射结论无条件推广到正特征的域。

### 两元素群

对 $S_2=\{e,\sigma\}$，$\sigma^2=e$，取

$$
\varphi(e)=I_2,\qquad \varphi(\sigma)=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

后者平方为 $I_2$，所以满足定义关系，给出同态；两只矩阵不同，所以单射。因 $S_2\cong\mu_2\cong\mathbb Z_2$，同样的两只矩阵也实现这两种写法的群。

### 三次单位根群的两种维数

令 $\mu_3=\langle\omega\mid\omega^3=1\rangle$，其中 $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$。取

$$
\varphi(\omega)=P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&1\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\varphi(\omega^2)=P^2=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\varphi(1)=I_3.
$$

有 $P^3=I_3$，且三只矩阵互异，故这是单射同态 $\mu_3\to GL(3,\kappa)$。本页直接按讲义使用这只 $P$；它将 $e_1\mapsto e_3,e_2\mapsto e_1,e_3\mapsto e_2$，与 [[Group/1. Foundations/0.1 Permutations|置换矩阵]] 中相反方向三循环的矩阵互为逆。

另一种实现只需一维：$\psi:\mu_3\to GL(1,\mathbb C)$，$\psi(\omega^r)=\omega^r$。它同样单射。由此可见矩阵大小不是抽象群本身的固有维数，同一个有限群可以有不同维的矩阵实现。

### 正方形的二面体群

使用 [[Group/1. Foundations/1. Basic Definitions and Examples#二面体群|二面体群]] 的展示 $D_4=\langle A,B\mid A^4=B^2=(AB)^2=1\rangle$，令

$$
\varphi(A)=M=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\varphi(B)=F=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

$M$ 是 $90^\circ$ 转动，$F$ 是关于第一坐标轴的反射。直接算得 $M^2=-I_2$、$F^2=I_2$、$MF=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$，故 $M^4=F^2=(MF)^2=I_2$，满足全部关系，给出同态。其像为

$$
\operatorname{im}\varphi=\{\pm I_2,\pm M,\pm F,\pm MF\}.
$$

八只矩阵互异，而 $|D_4|=8$，所以同态单射，$D_4$ 与这八只矩阵组成的群同构。若改将 $A,B$ 都送到 $I_2$，关系仍成立，但得到平凡同态，核为整个 $D_4$。

这四个例子都按“指定映射、检查关系、计算核或区分像”完成。更一般的表示分类是原课程第 7 章的问题，本页不提前引入其结构。
