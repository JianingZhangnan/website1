>[!def] $G/N$
>The set of cosets of a normal subgroup $N$ of group $G$ is often denoted by $G /N$.

For example, let $N$ be the integer multiples of $2\pi$, which is a subgroup of $\mathbb{R}^{+}$:
$$
N=\{\dots,-2\pi, 0,2\pi,4\boldsymbol{\pi},\dots  \}
$$
Then $G /N$ consists of the cosets $\theta+N$ of $N$ in $G$. In this example we could see $G /N$ made a sense that, it "compressed" all elements in $N$ to identity, so that $\theta+2n\pi$ are all regarded as the same.

> [!def] 陪集运算与映射的良定义性（Well-defined）
> 当通过陪集的代表元来定义商集或商群上的运算、映射（如商群乘法 $(aN)(bN)=abN$、或商群作用 $(gH)\cdot x = g\cdot x$）时，**良定义（Well-defined）**特指：**所得结果不依赖于陪集代表元的选取**。
>
> 即若选取了同一陪集的不同代表元 $a_1, a_2 \in aN$（即 $a_1 N = a_2 N$），必须证明导出的计算结果完全相同。
